# Demand-side checks

From *Demand-Side Sales 101* (Bob Moesta). Our wording, written as checks.

People hire a website to make progress out of a **struggling moment**. The job post is what they did after that moment. Find the moment.

## Four forces

Fill each from the client's own words, or mark it **unknown**.

| Force | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Push** | What about today became bad enough to act on? |
| **Pull** | What better day do they picture? |
| **Anxiety** | What worries them about changing? Cost, data, staff learning it, a past freelancer who let them down. |
| **Habit** | What keeps them doing it the old way? |

They switch only when push + pull outweigh anxiety + habit. Removing anxiety closes that gap faster than adding pull.

## Trigger

Something moved them from "this is annoying" to "post a job today": a new location, a staff member quitting, a bad review, a busy season. When the post and the reply leave it unnamed, ask.

## Checks

- **Struggling moment** has a time and a place: "Monday 7am, the front desk phone won't stop".
- **The ask and the progress are separate.** "New WordPress plugin" is the ask. "Members stop calling to book" is the progress.
- **Vague words are unpacked.** "Fast", "modern", "simple" each get a reference point: faster than what, simpler for whom.
