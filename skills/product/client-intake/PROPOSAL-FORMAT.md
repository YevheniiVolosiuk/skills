# Proposal format

[`ONE-SCREEN-FORMAT.md`](ONE-SCREEN-FORMAT.md) with one section added after Risk. Passes every check in [`WIN-WITHOUT-PITCHING.md`](WIN-WITHOUT-PITCHING.md).

The Risk section answers each anxiety from the intake.

```markdown
## Options

| | You get | Price | Ready |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | <smallest outcome that helps> | €<n> | <date> |
| **B: recommended** | <the outcome that fixes the pain> | €<n> | <date> |
| C | <B plus the next outcome> | €<n> | <date> |

<One line: why B.>

Reply with A, B or C and a start date that suits you.
```
